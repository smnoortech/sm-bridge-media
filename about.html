WHO WE ARE

An Agency Built Around Connections.

SM Bridge Media is a Nigeria-based influencer marketing and creator partnerships agency with an international outlook and an initial specialization in technology, gadgets and digital products.

We operate as an intermediary between companies seeking creator-led promotion and content creators interested in suitable brand partnership opportunities.

Our work focuses on discovering relevant creators, facilitating brand relationships, supporting partnership negotiations, coordinating campaigns and encouraging transparent working arrangements.

Our initial target markets include the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Nigeria and the United Arab Emirates, with a broader ambition to build relationships across international markets.

As we grow, our priority is to establish genuine relationships with creators and brands, understand their objectives and develop partnerships built on professionalism and mutual value.

OUR MISSION

OUR APPROACH

Partnerships Built on Trust.

We believe successful influencer marketing begins with understanding both sides of a partnership.

Brands need creators whose content, audience and communication style align with their objectives. Creators need opportunities that respect their work, audience and agreed terms.

Our approach is to bring these interests together through careful discovery, clear communication, transparent negotiations and organized campaign coordination.
