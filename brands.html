FOR COMPANIES

A Connection Between Your Brand and the Right Creators.

Finding suitable creators for a technology product requires more than comparing follower counts.

Content relevance, audience geography, average views, engagement quality, creator professionalism and campaign objectives all contribute to a meaningful partnership decision.

SM Bridge Media aims to help technology companies identify suitable creator partnership opportunities, facilitate discussions and coordinate agreed promotional campaigns.

We take a research-led approach to creator discovery, considering available profile information and the specific requirements of each campaign.

Creator availability and campaign capacity depend on verified profiles, creator permission and mutually agreed terms. We do not represent that a particular creator is available until this has been confirmed.

OUR SELECTION APPROACH

Finding the Right Creator Fit

We consider several factors when researching potential creator partnerships.

01

Content Relevance

How closely a creator's content aligns with the brand's product, industry and campaign message.

02

Audience Alignment

Whether the creator's audience demographics and geographic distribution match the brand's target market.

03

Engagement & Performance

Available views, engagement indicators and other relevant performance information, where verifiable.

04

Professional Fit

Communication, content quality, reliability and suitability for the proposed campaign.

CAMPAIGN POSSIBILITIES

Explore Different Ways to Work With Creators.

Depending on your objectives and available creator partnerships, potential campaign formats may include:

Sponsored content: Paid creator posts, videos and promotional integrations.

Product reviews: Product demonstrations, reviews and hands-on content.

Product launches: Creator-led promotional activities for new products.

Software demonstrations: Content introducing AI tools, applications and digital services.

Long-term collaborations: Potential ongoing creator relationships where both parties agree to the arrangement.

All campaign formats are subject to creator availability, suitability, permission and agreed commercial terms.
