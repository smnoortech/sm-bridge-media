WORK WITH US

We Want to Discover Your Work.

SM Bridge Media is developing an international network of technology and digital products creators, with a focus on building professional relationships between creators and relevant brands.

We welcome enquiries from creators producing original content on platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Twitch and LinkedIn.

Our objective is to identify suitable paid promotional opportunities and facilitate partnerships that align with a creator's content, audience, values and preferences.

We understand that creators invest time and effort in building their platforms. We aim to approach potential collaborations with clear communication, respect for creator independence and transparent partnership terms.

Submitting an enquiry does not guarantee representation, sponsorships or paid campaigns. Each potential relationship is considered individually, and any representation arrangement requires mutual agreement.
